1. **`ac report <run-dir>`** (`src/report/`): a static HTML bundle per run, offline and deterministic. It contains:
   - the before/after folder graph (`graph.json`, derived from the base and head trees), shown as a files-flow and two adjacency matrices;
   - the ladder with an expandable certificate per step, failed attempts, rollbacks, and the rung and whole-PR certificates;
   - the findings, the table-access heatmap, the cost ledger by budget run, and the A/B.
   Every sentence about a run is built from that run's data. The bundle for immich is 2 MB.
2. **`ac site`** (`src/site/`): the landing page, one report per `runs/*`, the A/B page, the benchmarks page and "verify it yourself". `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys it. It is served for review at `http://claude-runner.tail1b9417.ts.net:8707/`.
3. **`ac demo`** (`src/demo/`). The fast path is decided: replay the plan, re-execute every move step, compare every tree with the recording, regenerate R4, static-certify a sample, and replay the narration.
   - `--env <checkout> --oracles --work <dir outside the clone>` reproduces the recorded certificate digests byte for byte.
   - `--full` runs the 2-hour ladder.
   - `--live` asks Nemotron on the judge's own key, read from the environment only and capped at $1.
   - The README draft says what the replay proves and what it does not.
4. **The seeded wrong move** (`runs/immich-fault`, 13 m 37 s on a quiet machine):
   - `--inject-fault 2:M2` drops one importer rewrite, and K3a, K3b and K3c fail on it;
   - the attempt is rolled back, `album.dto.ts` is pinned and the step is replanned, then passes;
   - the partial R1 certificate passes with the oracles;
   - step 1's certificate reproduces S6's digest `909262855d`.
5. **`ac scramble`** (`src/scramble/`): our executor flattens a feature-modular repo into type folders in one commit, and our certifier checks that commit. H1 gives opaque file names.
   - Profiles: hoppscotch, kaneo and ghostfolio.
   - Executor gaps it found, now fixed: the `alias-rewrite-hazard` decline, Jest `testRegex` modelled, `*.config.*` files as configs.
   - Static certificates: hoppscotch, H1 and ghostfolio PASS. kaneo FAILs closed: its runtime is an esbuild bundle K3c doesn't model, and K4 can't prove two URL templates aren't paths. K8 passed on all four in development.
6. **`ac bench`** (`src/bench/`): MoJoFM, ARI, c2c_cvg and the cluster ratio. The metrics are checked against ARCADE's oracle values. The baselines are B0, name-token, export-token, both seeds drafts and Louvain r=1/2/4; they reproduce G1 on its inputs. Real targets get TurboMQ and back-edges.
   - MoJoFM, planner vs the best deterministic method: hoppscotch **87.59** vs 81.38; H1 79.31 vs 81.38; kaneo 49.61 vs 66.90 (Louvain r=4); ghostfolio 82.64 vs 84.72.
   - immich, TurboMQ: 10.07 vs 10.50.
   - **The planner wins 1 of 5.** On ghostfolio and kaneo the winning Ultra edit named files by id, so the validator dropped every move and the planner kept its draft. The results say so per entry.
7. **Certifier precision from dogfooding**, round 2 plus the review fixes:
   - **K4** judges a would-be violation by what the syntax around it proves: route decorators, test titles, matchers, prose keys, location arithmetic, Nix lock inputs. It never clears a comparison with an exact moved path.
   - The reachability rule (`unreachable-path-string`) is the one judgment call. It names its residual in every finding: a consumer outside the repo that creates files under an emptied folder.
   - **K3c:** Nest's rewrites are probed before the runtime's preloads, and the probe runs in the resolution skeleton. A test-runner CLI is K3b's, except mocha, which keeps node hooks. A bundled launch fails closed.
   - **K2** accepts `vi`/`jest` only when the environment proves the re-export.
   - immich's certificates are unchanged on 6 ranges. The mutation harness kills **420/420**, including 2 new hunt cases from the review.
8. **S6 carry-overs closed:**
   - K8 runs are cached per tree, keyed by content, so a rung's base reuses the previous head and the whole PR reuses R4's head. Digests are identical with and without the cache.
   - A certificate that only errored is resumable, unless an errored check carries violations.
   - Critic prompt 2 states its gate's rules. immich pins prompt 1 for replays only.
   - `testRegex` is modelled.
   - A profile's oracle quarantine follows the moves.
